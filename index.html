About

Hi, I'm
Cassandra Lacroix.

Born in the eastern townships of Quebec, I now call Montreal my home. I've been doing photography for over 15 years now, shooting a mix of music, portrait, landscape and lifestyle. It's not easy to define my style — I'm still trying to find my niche after all this time.

I like to find beauty in everything around me; this is what drew me to photography. Whether it is shooting at a show, capturing memories during a trip or highlighting the authenticity of a person, I always find a joy in it.

I love listening to people's stories, pretending to be a barista and listening to music.
