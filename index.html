Cassandra Lacroix

Photographer · Montréal

Born in the eastern townships of Quebec, I now call Montreal home. For over 15 years, I've been shooting music, portraits, landscapes, and lifestyle work. Rather than boxing myself into a single niche, I've built a practice that thrives on range.

I'm drawn to photography because I see beauty everywhere. Whether I'm working a show, documenting a trip, or capturing the authenticity of a person, I bring the same energy and eye to every frame.

Off camera, I love hearing people's stories, playing barista, and getting lost in music.
